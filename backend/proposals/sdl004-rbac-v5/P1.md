# SDL-004B P1 remediation

Policy source is [v5.1 at the owner-approved amendment](../../../docs/SDL-004_Account_Roles_and_Permission_Amendment_v5.1.md). The three P1 migrations follow the original v5 candidate in timestamp order.

## Authentication and caller inventory

| Path | Enforcement |
|---|---|
| Private statistics, pending performance, family and registration fields, audit history | Platform grant and AAL1 or AAL2 for viewing. Read RPCs enforce resource scope and retain narrow response contracts. The platform view path rejects CSV negotiation at AAL1. |
| Clubhouse roster, leaderboard and sessions | Platform viewing assurance plus the existing team, content classification and consent tests. |
| Video lists, old playback permission RPC, v2 playback and compatible v1 playback | Object authorization rechecks session, account, rights, subject and retention. The Worker uses the caller JWT and rechecks each range request. Successful Platform Admin playback records an authorization event. |
| Video attachment, official statistics export by platform or player, private history export | Platform-derived authorization requires AAL2. An independently valid scoped role may still authorize its ordinary equivalent. The Worker sends the attachment only after the download RPC approves and records the request. |
| Club Admin delegation, verified global unlink, case-bound support and identity correction, platform photo eligibility | Existing stronger AAL2 helper is retained. Routine non-platform role authorization remains independent. |
| Grant provisioning, recovery and verified consent operations | Private operator boundary. Recovery requires independent verification and audit through the identity provider's controlled recovery workflow. An ordinary password reset or an SDL club role supplies no AAL2 claim. Review MFA enrollment, recovery and revocation with the real provider before activation. |
| Direct RLS and legacy routes | A restrictive session/account policy intersects existing core RLS. Verify that hosted RLS is enabled and that no alternate storage or exposed schema grants remain. First-profile invitation redemption retains a narrow verified-email and valid-session path. |

Viewing and playback deliver private information to clients. A viewer may copy JSON responses, take screenshots, record playback or save bytes, including chunks delivered through range requests. Attachment and export controls govern the product operations. Revoking a session prevents new authorized requests and cannot recall previously delivered content.

## Historical scope and consent

P1-02 uses independently verified, record-level assignment evidence. Staff must still qualify in the original team and role. Historical-only staff access covers matching private reads and eligible playback. Original-club administrators can read and export retained source-club history. Guardian and staff media access after enrollment ends requires evidence bound to the original video and enrollment generation. Keep ambiguous records and footage private. No assignment evidence or media rights are seeded. Reconcile creation timestamps and staff assignment intervals against authoritative records before entering evidence.

For P1-04, run the private read-only `sdl_rbac_v5.consent_review_inventory()` in a controlled disposable clone. Reconcile original terms, purpose, separate media rights, receipts, guardians, enrollment generations and withdrawal events. Keep the original receipts. Reviewed equivalent statistics scope may be recorded with explicitly approved metrics and a review reference. The write records an audit event. Missing evidence, invalidated receipts and unresolved objections block expansion. Video review classification records evidence for reconciliation. Additional video uses still require the separate recording, access, subject and retention controls. No historic consent is automatically broadened by installation.

Guardian objections are tracked separately from preferences. A new opt-in can express a preference but cannot clear an unresolved objection. The operator-only resolution requires independent verification and records an immutable resolution and audit event. Review legacy evidence before activation, including objections superseded in older preference data.
