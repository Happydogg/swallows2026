## Pilot behavior

- An organization administrator or staff member with an explicit `guardian_invite` scope selects an enrolled athlete and invites a specific email. The Worker generates a random 256-bit token; PostgreSQL stores only its SHA-256 hash. Invitations expire after 72 hours and are single-use, revocable, enrollment-generation-bound and audited. Issuer membership changes invalidate outstanding invitations permanently; invitation-history contact reads are logged. Knowing a name or jersey cannot link an account. A stolen token cannot redeem under a different or unconfirmed email.
- WhatsApp only shares a link through an explicit user click. The token is in a URL fragment, stripped immediately and held only in memory until sign-in. Nothing is sent automatically. The parent signs in using email/password or creates an email account, confirms email and signs in, then accepts the invitation. No OTP provider is added. Reopening the original link after email confirmation is supported; tokens are not persisted through browser restarts.
- Redemption creates/reactivates the verified guardian relationship, preserves an existing coach role and adds current parent-team assignments where absent. An internal team transfer adds an absent current-team parent assignment without reactivating an explicitly revoked assignment. Server checks also require a currently linked child in that exact club/team; an old parent-team row alone is insufficient. Guardian links are athlete-wide; sharing permissions remain independent per club enrollment.
- Verified guardians can read their own child's limited family information and private growth/performance history. Assigned coaches can read baseball/growth/performance projections, never general family contacts. Private family history remains available after a club departure; former coaches lose enrollment-dependent performance/growth access.
- Parents and assigned coaches record height/weight directly. Canonical units are cm/kg; conversion from inches/pounds occurs at the Worker, with bounds checked again in SQL. Corrections require the expected revision and a reason. Previous values, date and recording actor/role remain in immutable history. Growth rows reuse `sessions` so existing private charts continue to work. Legacy growth rows display as unattributed, rather than inventing an actor.
- Parent baseball measurements enter a private pending queue. The existing coach review page contains the performance inbox alongside video reviews. Assigned coaches approve, reject or request correction, with evidence and immutable events. A stale revision returns 409. Parents cannot edit approved measurements; a correction is a new pending proposal. An authorized coach may enter an official result directly, with attribution. A parent/coach dual-role account must explicitly choose coach entry; it cannot review its own parent submission.
- An assigned coach explicitly reviews/approves the minimal baseball display profile; guardian consent alone cannot publish it. A source profile change, rejoin or explicit revocation invalidates that approval. Reverting a source edit cannot revive an old approval. No historical profiles are auto-approved.
- Only official results with an explicit active protocol and a staff-approved baseball profile can rank. Every athlete appears once per protocol group using their latest valid official measurement, then results rank by value (sprint ascending, other metrics descending). Sprint distance and timing method are separate groups. Standing broad jump uses absolute cm. Jump/height ratio remains private. Historical raw sessions and old protocol-free approvals are not automatically made leaderboard-eligible.
- Protocol registry: radar exit/throw velocity in km/h; standing broad jump in cm; standing-start sprint at 18.29 m hand/electronic timing, and 27.43 m hand timing, in seconds. Changing a testing method requires a separately versioned protocol, not relabeling existing results.
- Parents see **Approve All Clubhouse Sharing**, **Withdraw All Clubhouse Sharing**, and advanced individual choices. Approval requires an explicit disclosure checkbox. The atomic RPC updates independent video receipts and baseball profile/metric choices; defaults are off. It never grants recording/storage permission or approves media/statistics. No height, weight, family contact, private history or unapproved result is shared. Old ratio choices never imply consent to absolute jump distance.
- Sharing remains scoped to one athlete/club/enrollment generation. Internal transfers preserve consent but visibility follows current teams. Departure/inactivation invalidates consent permanently; same-ID activation or rejoin requires fresh consent. Withdrawal preserves private videos and measurements. Withdrawal does not recall copies already downloaded by an authorized viewer; fresh Worker requests are denied and the existing Clubhouse revalidation clears playback.
- Publication is limited to a coach's explicit single-athlete safety attestation. Multiple/unknown identifiable athletes remain private. This is a human review safeguard, not automated face/subject detection. Publication and team playback require approval, the current video receipt, current membership and recording/storage evidence. v1 private upload/list/playback routes are unchanged.
- Team communication and registration contacts require explicit, auditable grants, scoped to a team or (registration only) club. Generic staff, coach or administrator labels grant no automatic family-contact access. Team-scoped grants also need a current team assignment. Membership identity/role/status changes permanently deactivate grants until an administrator explicitly renews them. Contact access is logged; no home address, identity documents, growth or private performance/media are returned through these APIs.
